# Training Log

One entry per week. Five minutes to write, and the only thing that reliably catches drift.
Newest at the top.

Copy the template. Be honest in the "what I avoided" line — that's the one that's actually
worth writing, because the thing you keep skipping is usually the thing you most need.

---

## Template

```
## Week of YYYY-MM-DD  ·  Block N

Reps closed:        <count, and which tracks>
Biggest thing I learned:
Something that surprised me:
What I got wrong / had to redo:
What I avoided this week, and why:
Artifact I'm proudest of:
Next week's focus (pick ONE track as primary):
```
