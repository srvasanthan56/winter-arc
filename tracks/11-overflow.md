# Track 11 — Overflow (staging, work, random)

> **This is not a training track.** It does not count toward overall progress.
> **Never "done."** You add lines here when something real happens that is not on tracks 01–10.
> **Do not plan weeks around this file.** Open it when staging breaks, a ticket appears, or you poke at something off-syllabus.

## Why this exists

Tracks 01–10 are the gym. Life is not only the gym. You will debug a staging deploy, chase a
weird prod-adjacent bug, try a tool because a coworker mentioned it, or spend a Saturday on
something that does not fit a set. If that work has nowhere to go, it either disappears or
it pollutes the syllabus with boxes that were never part of the program.

This file is the parking lot. Log the thing. Check it when you are done. If it later looks
like real training, move the lesson into the right track's `notes/` and leave a one-line
pointer here.

**Code and dumps** go in `work/11-overflow/<short-name>/`.
**Writeups** go in `notes/` only if you will reread them. Most overflow items do not need a note.

## How to add an item

Copy a line into the matching section. One line, one thing, dated if you want.

```markdown
- [ ] 2026-09-22 · staging · payment timeout on staging-api, need a repro
```

Check it when the thing is actually finished, not when you "looked at it."
Promote it if it belongs on 01–10: do the real set, then strike or check this line with
`(moved to Track 03)` so you do not do it twice.

Do not invent boxes in advance to make this look full. Empty sections are the healthy state.
